# Player intelligence workspace

Mode: Operate. The user's confirmed priority is player exploration and comparison.

FORM: scouting desk; seed:8ee8ea87a4f04a8b. Code-first application screen.

## Direction contract

The first viewport must read as an NBA analytics application, with familiar navigation, a clear current scope, a linked plot, and a recognizable player. Use warm paper, a pale olive navigation rail, deep forest ink, and court-orange actions. Use Manrope for controls and tabular numbers, and Barlow Condensed only for player display names and team monograms.

The signature is a photograph-led scouting spotlight: oversize jersey numerals behind a recognizable player headshot, with three compact stats and direct profile/compare controls. Preserve the utility of the sortable table and conventional controls. Selected players persist in a comparison dock.

At desktop widths, keep navigation visible and put spotlight and scatter next to each other. At small widths, use a navigation drawer, stack the panels, and confine horizontal scrolling to data tables. Native profile dialogs must support Escape and focus containment. Real snapshot source, observed coverage, retrieval time, and season completeness must remain visible.

## Grounded directions considered

1. Basketball reference annual: warm reference paper, compact ink tables, orange tabs.
2. Courtside broadcast: charcoal, chalk white, signal yellow, score typography.
3. Team media guide: white stock, midnight ink, blue annotations, roster portraits.
4. Hardwood practice gym: honey, cream, evergreen, restrained condensed lettering.
5. Scouting desk: olive paper, forest ink, court orange, portrait-led player notes.
6. Basketball magazine index: white, red, blackened ink, strong typographic columns.
7. Community court signage: chalk white, asphalt, cobalt, clear pictogram navigation.

The fifth direction governed this implementation. Catalog alternatives were reviewed against the task: annual plates support clear alignment but offer less basketball identification; garden-map forms obscure dense statistics; boarding-pass and split-flap forms contribute no stronger player comparison model; star-atlas notation risks chart-first navigation; acetate tabs add unnecessary layering. The selected direction retains their useful discipline: aligned rules, explicit labels, readable point fields, and unmistakable active controls. It does not borrow their decorative motifs.

This direction was an implementation decision, not a claim of explicit user approval of a visual comp. No generated comp was used. No standing image-generation workflow preference was recorded.
